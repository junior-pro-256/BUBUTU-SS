Foundation & Early Years

How Bubutu Secondary School began

Bubutu Secondary School was established in 1989 by the Anglican Church of Makhonge as a community-based institution, operating with only two classes — S.1 and S.2 — because of limited space and classroom blocks. Its founding purpose was to expand access to secondary education for children from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

At that time there was only Bumbo SS and Magale SS, making it difficult for children from places like Butiru, Nabitsikhi, Namboko and Bubutu to access post-primary education — leaving a significant gap that the school was founded to fill.

Leadership

Leadership & Administration

Since it was established, Bubutu Secondary School has been led by a series of dedicated headteachers who have contributed to its stability and development. The longest-serving headteacher was Mr. Mungau Patrick, the founder headteacher, who managed to serve the institution for 11 years (1989 to 2000) and laid a strong academic foundation.

Our Legacy

Growth, Legacy & Academic Excellence

Bubutu Secondary School has a rich history rooted in community service, access to education and sustained academic development. Since its establishment, the school has grown from a modest community initiative into a respected government-aided secondary school serving learners across the Bugisu sub-region and beyond.

2011

Transition To Government-Aided Status

Bubutu Secondary School transitioned into a government-aided institution in 2011, fully supported and regulated by the Ministry of Education and Sports. This enhanced access to public resources, teacher deployment and national education programmes, aligning the school with Uganda Vision 2040 and subsequent national development goals.

2014

Academic Growth & Expansion

The school initially offered Ordinary Level (O-Level) education only. Due to its growing reputation, discipline and academic performance, Bubutu Secondary School was upgraded in 2014 to also offer Advanced Level (A-Level) education — a significant milestone enabling learners to pursue higher academic pathways within their local community.

10 → 2,300

Enrollment Growth

Enrollment steadily increased over the years under the leadership of Mr. Kimono Paul, reflecting growing trust from parents and improved academic outcomes. From an initial enrollment of 10 students and only two teachers, the school has now grown to 2,300 learners as of 2026.

Today

Bubutu Secondary School Today

Today, Bubutu Secondary School stands as a respected and best-performing government-aided, mixed-day, O-Level and A-Level secondary school in the region, with a serene learning environment, committed staff, and ongoing infrastructure development.
