Who We Are

A community built on academic excellence

Welcome to the official website of Bubutu Secondary School. This is where academic excellence, discipline, innovation, and character development come together to prepare learners for a successful future.

We are committed to providing affordable, quality education in a safe and supportive environment that enables every learner to achieve their full potential. Our dedicated staff work closely with parents and the community to nurture responsible, self-reliant, and God-fearing citizens.

We invite you to explore our website and learn more about the opportunities available at Bubutu Secondary School.

Admissions are open. Join us today and be part of our journey towards excellence.

Why Choose Us

Why join us today

Bubutu Secondary School stands out as an inspiration of educational excellence in both Uganda and the Eastern region — known for its strong commitment to academic achievement, talent growth, innovation and holistic development. Here, students strive academically while also growing in character, developing leadership skills and talent, and achieving personal growth in a supportive and nurturing environment.
