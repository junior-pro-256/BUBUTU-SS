Who We Are

A learner-centred school in Namisindwa District

Bubutu Secondary School is a mixed Ordinary Level and Advanced Level secondary school located in Bubutu, Namisindwa District, approximately 400m from Bubutu Trading Centre along the Mbale–Lwakhakha Highway.

Our school provides a learner-centred education that combines academic excellence with moral values, leadership development, creativity, innovation, and practical life skills. We strive to create a learning environment where every student is valued, challenged, and supported to succeed.

Curriculum

Academics

Our curriculum follows the Uganda National Curriculum and prepares learners for success in the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) and Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) examinations. We offer both Arts and Science programs, supported by experienced teachers and continuous assessment to help learners reach their academic goals.
